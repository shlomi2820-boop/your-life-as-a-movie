GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
before the credits.

Clear expectations matter. Here's what this early version can — and can't — do.

Is the movie generator live?

Not yet. This website currently demonstrates the concept, the sample poster and the planned packages. AI photo/video generation and delivery still need to be built and connected.

Can I pay $3 or $9.99 right now?

No. The prices are planned concept prices. The checkout on this page is a visual preview only and cannot charge a card or accept PayPal.

Will I need to upload a photo?

The intended product is a personalized film starring the customer. A real photo-upload flow, consent, secure processing and deletion controls must be implemented before launch.

When will my movie arrive?

Delivery timing will be published once the generation and fulfillment process is working reliably. No delivery promise is being made in this prototype.
